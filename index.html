THE EXECUTIVE CYBER RANGE

Lead through the crisis.
Before it becomes real.

A facilitated tabletop for the CISO, the CIO and Legal. Each inject arrives with a clock: pick a decision, say who you tell, and watch the simulated consequences. At the end you get a scored report, role by role, with the playbook references behind every call.

All organisations, people, amounts and systems are fictional. Nothing leaves your browser.

02 crisis scenarios15 decision injects03 executive roles01 actionable debrief
1. Choose a scenario
2. Assign the roles

One shared screen, one facilitator. Roles are decision owners, not separate online player accounts. Use initials; names are optional and appear in exported reports.

3. Set the pressure

When the clock runs out, the inject resolves as "no decision". Pause is facilitator-controlled. Resuming after a reload restarts the current inject's clock.
