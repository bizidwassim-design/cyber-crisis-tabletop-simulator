## Improvement actions

- **R2 · CIO:** With a domain admin compromised, assume email and chat are read: move to an out-of-band channel. See [Privileged account compromise · D1, D3, D4, D5](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/privileged-account-compromise.md#4-decision-points).
- **R5 · CIO:** Recover by business priority and publish the order; parallel restores starve the systems that matter. See [Malware outbreak · D4](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/malware-outbreak.md#4-decision-points), [Privileged account compromise · D5](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/privileged-account-compromise.md#4-decision-points).
- **R6 · Legal counsel:** Add Data protection regulator to the notification list for this decision.
