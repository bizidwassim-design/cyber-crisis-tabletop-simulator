## Improvement actions

- **B1 · Legal counsel:** In payment fraud the first hours decide what can be recovered; call the bank on suspicion. See [Data breach · D7 (law enforcement)](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/data-breach-personal-data.md#4-decision-points), [Supply chain · D1](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/supply-chain-vendor-compromise.md#4-decision-points).
- **B3 · CIO:** No decision was made in time: Close the access path the attacker used for every account that shares it, then verify with sign-in monitoring. See [Privileged account compromise · D4](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/privileged-account-compromise.md#4-decision-points).
- **B3 · CIO:** Add All staff to the notification list for this decision.
- **B4 · Legal counsel:** Add Cyber insurer to the notification list for this decision.
- **B6 · CISO:** Warn the people who were actually targeted, directly. See [Supply chain · D5](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/supply-chain-vendor-compromise.md#4-decision-points), [Data breach · communication templates](https://github.com/bizidwassim-design/incident-response-playbooks/blob/main/playbooks/data-breach-personal-data.md#6-communication-templates).
